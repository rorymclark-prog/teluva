# Teluva private beta — feedback survey

Use this as a short Google Form. Keep email collection off unless a tester agrees to a follow-up.

## Introduction

Thank you for helping test Teluva. Please spend 10–15 minutes trying one real family or business task, starting with non-sensitive information. This survey takes about three minutes. Honest criticism is more useful than politeness.

## Questions

1. **Which space did you test?**  
   Multiple choice: Family Hub / Business Hub / Both

2. **How easy was it to understand what to do first?**  
   Linear scale: 1 — I felt lost / 5 — It was immediately clear

3. **What felt most useful or valuable?**  
   Short answer

4. **What confused you, felt unfinished, or did not work?**  
   Paragraph answer

5. **Would you use Teluva again with your own family or business? Why or why not?**  
   Paragraph answer

6. **What one change would make you most likely to return?**  
   Short answer

## Optional follow-up

**May Rory contact you about your answers?**  
Multiple choice: Yes / No

If yes, ask for their preferred WhatsApp number or email in a separate optional field.
